## 👋 Behind acarsec

I'm **Ebu Bekir Acar**, a cybersecurity analyst with a strong blue team foundation, actively developing my skills in **offensive security, red teaming, bug bounty research, and Python-based security tooling**. I'm pursuing a master's degree in cybersecurity and practice through hands-on training on TryHackMe and Hack The Box.

[**acarsec.github.io**](https://acarsec.github.io/) brings my security portfolio and technical writing together. It is where I share cybersecurity fundamentals, document learning, and build toward more detailed investigations and research.

My interests connect **network security, threat intelligence, incident response, and OSINT** with both offensive and defensive perspectives.
